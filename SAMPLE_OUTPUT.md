# Sample output (illustrative)

This is an example of the kind of story the app can generate. It is a hand-written sample for preview; the local model may produce different output.

## The Last Login

At 12:07 a.m., the old lab computer woke itself.

Mira had shut it down before leaving. She remembered the click of the power button, the dark monitor, the reflection of the exit sign disappearing from the glass. Yet now a pale cursor blinked in the centre of the screen.

A login prompt appeared.

**USER: ELIAS**

Nobody had used that account since the lab's former technician died the previous winter. His account had been deleted. His desk had been cleared. Even the mug with the chipped blue handle was gone.

Mira typed `who are you?`

The cursor paused, then answered.

`still waiting for the build to finish`

She knew that sentence. Elias had said it every evening, laughing at the ancient machine that took an hour to compile a program. The final build had never finished. The power failed on the night he died, leaving the progress bar at ninety-nine percent.

The fan spun up. On the screen, a progress bar appeared—ninety-nine percent.

Mira reached for the power cable.

A new line appeared before she could touch it.

`you can go home. i'll keep watch.`

The progress bar moved to one hundred.

The monitor went black. In its reflection, for one impossible second, Mira saw a man standing behind her, holding a chipped blue mug.

In the morning, the computer was off.

The build log showed a successful completion timestamped at 12:08 a.m.—the first one in eleven months.
